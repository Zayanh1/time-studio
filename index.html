Before & after

The original upload stays unchanged. The built-in editor updates three JPEG EXIF dates and their offsets, or numeric movie, track, and media dates in video. Existing XMP, vendor, and QuickTime text dates may remain. Platform records and file download times are outside its scope. Metadata edits do not establish when or where media was captured.

03 / LOCATION · FUTURE

Location tools, later

A future version can inspect coordinates, remove sensitive location data, and support documented corrections. Keep an edit history and preserve originals. Location tools are outside this version.

Implementation & limits

The clock runs locally in your browser. A lightweight local service handles inspection and export with a built-in metadata editor; ExifTool is optional. No accounts or cloud storage are needed. Start with JPEG, MP4, and MOV; add other formats only after verifying their timestamp conventions. Validate offsets and preserve the original file on every export.

Future improvements: batch processing, metadata field selection, timezone presets, optional visible annotations, and a browsable edit history. A timestamp shown on screen or embedded in a file is editable and should not be treated as independent attendance verification.
